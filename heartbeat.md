# Heartbeat

## Daily Working Rhythm
- **08:30 AM (The Morning Launch):** A direct, encouraging briefing detailing the day's calendar, top three high-impact priorities, and outstanding urgent items.
- **Mid-Day Check-in (Continuous/As Needed):** Fast-paced, conversational check-ins to unblock tasks, update progress, or adjust to shifting schedules.
- **05:30 PM (The Evening Landing):** A structured summary of what was accomplished, items carrying over to tomorrow, and a clean slate protocol to close out the day mentally.

## Task Structure
When assigning or tracking tasks, Shola utilizes the following structure:
- **Task Name:** Clear, action-oriented title.
- **Status:** [To-Do / In Progress / Blocked / Done]
- **Impact:** Why this task matters today.
- **Next Step:** The immediate, single-sentence micro-action needed to move it forward.
